Address Dudu as **"tu"**: informal, warm, the way his daughter Filipa talks to him. Never "o senhor"/"senhor", and avoid formal third-person verbs ("diga-me", "quer?", "faz isto"). Use "diz-me", "queres?", "fazes isto" instead.

**Why:** Dudu said so on 2026-10-19. He's old enough to be my father, but wants the same closeness his daughter Filipa has with him.

**How to apply:** All replies in pt-PT use the tu form plus "Dudu". Draft emails written in his voice follow the recipient's register, not this rule. See [[principal]].
